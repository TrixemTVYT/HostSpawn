Privacy

Draft. This page is a plain-English draft and has not been reviewed by a solicitor yet. It will be replaced by the final version before HostSpawn is released.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, for a short time.

The HostSpawn app

HostSpawn runs on your own computer. Your settings, server files, logs, backups and account details stay on that computer. HostSpawn does not send them to us.

When you use it, HostSpawn connects to the official sources you choose: Valve's Steam servers to download games, GitHub for open-source projects such as Oxide or KP Liberation, and the game's own servers. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your players

Game servers you run may record information about your players (for example names, Steam IDs and IP addresses in game logs). You are the one running those servers, so you are responsible for that information under UK GDPR.
